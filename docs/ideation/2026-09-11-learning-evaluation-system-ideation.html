A Learning Evaluation System for WelfareTech Explorer

The system does not need a perfect label for “good idea” before it can improve. It needs a ladder of increasingly consequential evidence, with objective checks near the bottom and delayed welfare outcomes at the top.

Grounding Context

The initial PRD already anticipates historical backtesting, known-intervention recovery, novelty benchmarks, citation accuracy, independent expert evaluation, configurable pipeline experiments, false-positive and false-negative tracking, and full research provenance. Its quality model is deliberately multidimensional: welfare impact, technical feasibility, adoption, neglectedness, scalability, additionality, evidence strength, and information value.

The principal gap is operationalisation. The document does not yet define benchmark cases, scoring anchors, reviewer calibration, the treatment of disagreement, stage-specific outcome labels, rejected-idea audits, or rules for promoting one pipeline configuration over another.

Core conclusion: final idea quality should be treated as a delayed, partly subjective latent outcome. Improvement should come from multiple linked measurements rather than a single composite reward.

The Outcome and Forecast Ladder

Every idea produces explicit forecasts for the next observable rung: prior-art survival, expert endorsement, technical validation, buyer interest, prototype performance, pilot behaviour change, and eventual welfare change. Each forecast has a deadline and resolution rule; the system scores calibration as evidence arrives and periodically tests which early signals actually predicted later rungs.

Axis
Confidence calibration and forecasting
Basis
direct: The PRD already stores confidence, evidence, counterevidence, stage decisions, timestamps, validation plans, and human decisions. external: Proper scoring rules and calibration curves allow probabilistic predictions to be evaluated before ultimate impact is known.
Rationale
This is the central learning engine. It converts every research stage into feedback without mistaking an early milestone for final welfare success.
Downsides
Resolution criteria require discipline; many predictions will censor or resolve slowly. Poorly chosen milestones could become proxies the system games, so correlations with later rungs must be audited.

2 · Blind-spot control

Rejection Insurance Pool

Randomly send a stratified 5–10% sample of rejected ideas through the next research stage, covering different species, technology families, pipeline gates, and rejection reasons. Compare the deeper result with the original kill decision to estimate false negatives and identify filters that systematically suppress unusual but valuable opportunities.

Axis
Anti-gaming and evaluation governance
Basis
direct: The PRD names false-negative rate, configurable stopping rules, recorded decisions, and a steep 1,000-to-1–3 funnel. reasoned: Survivor-only evaluation creates selection bias because incorrectly rejected ideas otherwise disappear.
Rationale
The costliest failure may be invisible: the pipeline confidently discarding its one exceptional idea. This creates labels for the failure rather than assuming it away.
Downsides
Consumes scarce research budget on candidates believed to be weak. Sampling must be large enough to be informative and protected from cherry-picking.

90%

3 · Hard-case benchmark

Adversarial Challenge Bank

Maintain versioned cases containing obscure patents, terminology traps, failed prototypes, persuasive unsupported claims, productivity-only benefits, and technologies that measure distress without causing action. Include cases whose correct answer is “insufficient evidence.” Score prior-art Recall@k, citation support, classification, causal-chain diagnosis, calibrated abstention, and robustness to irrelevant rewording.

Axis
Component correctness
Basis
direct: The PRD requires terminology expansion, a novelty taxonomy, citation auditing, an explicit welfare causal pathway, welfare/productivity separation, and uncertainty when searches are inconclusive. external: Information-retrieval benchmarks provide established recall and ranking measures.
Rationale
It turns several unprovable claims—especially “nothing similar exists”—into measurable capabilities under controlled cases.
Downsides
Building authoritative cases is labour-intensive. A visible static set will eventually be overfit, so a protected and rotating subset is needed.

4 · Human measurement

Reviewer Calibration Guild

Separate welfare-science, technical-feasibility, and adoption judgements. Reviewers first score shared anchor cases, then make blinded pairwise choices such as “which deserves the next £1,000 of research?” Track agreement and domain-specific reviewer reliability; route high disagreement into targeted research rather than averaging it away.

Axis
Expert judgement and reviewer reliability
Basis
direct: The PRD calls for independent expert ratings, human overrides, multidimensional scoring, and blinded comparison. external: Pairwise ranking and inter-rater reliability provide more stable signals than unanchored absolute scores.
Rationale
Expert judgement becomes a reusable measurement instrument, while disagreement becomes information about which assumption needs evidence.
Downsides
Experts are scarce and may share field-wide biases. Pairwise comparisons infer relative preference, not moral truth or causal impact.

86%

5 · End-to-end foresight

Time-Capsule Discovery League

Create historical evaluation worlds containing only evidence available before a cutoff date. Ask pipeline variants to rank opportunities that later became useful alongside contemporaneous dead ends, remove later terminology, check for date leakage, and retain unseen eras as holdouts.

Axis
Delayed outcomes and historical backtesting
Basis
direct: Historical cutoff backtesting and known-intervention recovery are already specified in the PRD. reasoned: Matched dead ends distinguish foresight from retrospective storytelling.
Rationale
This is the closest repeatable test of whether the system could have found tomorrow’s useful intervention yesterday.
Downsides
Outcome labels are contestable and reconstructing a clean historic information environment is difficult. Historical success may not transfer to new opportunity classes.
Confidence
80%
Complexity
High
6 · Improvement governance

Shadow Pipeline League

Run the current pipeline and challenger configurations on identical frozen batches. Compare the full system with ablated versions, a minimal one-agent workflow, and matched-budget human brainstorming; anonymise outputs and promote challengers only when repeated results improve the Pareto frontier without breaching evidence and welfare guardrails.

Axis
Anti-gaming and evaluation governance
Basis
direct: The PRD makes models, prompts, agents, strategies, and stopping rules configurable and proposes blinded control/treatment evaluation plus cost metrics. reasoned: Ablations make every added agent earn its cost.
Rationale
This provides the actual mechanism for continuous improvement: a challenger replaces the champion only when it demonstrably performs better.
Downsides
Requires repeated trials and enough expert judgements to overcome noise. A Pareto frontier still needs governance choices about guardrails and acceptable trade-offs.

7 · Debuggability

Error Cascade Map

Evaluate every handoff separately—retrieval, extraction, bottleneck classification, analogy generation, novelty search, causal-path analysis, scoring, and synthesis. Replay downstream stages with a corrected upstream input to estimate how much each component changes the final decision.

Axis
Component correctness
Basis
direct: The PRD defines structured agent outputs, a staged funnel, full provenance, and configurable components. reasoned: Counterfactual replay distinguishes the agent that introduced an error from the agents that merely inherited it.
Rationale
Without local evaluation, teams tend to tune the final judge even when the decisive failure was missing evidence several stages earlier.
Downsides
Gold intermediate records are expensive to author. Correcting one handoff may create an artificial input that downstream agents would not normally receive.
