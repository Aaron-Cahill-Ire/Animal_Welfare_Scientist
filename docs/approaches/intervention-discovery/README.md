# Intervention Discovery

> **Status:** Exploratory approach family; no method has been selected or
> validated

Use this family when the welfare problem is understood well enough to state a
required biological, physical, environmental or operational change.

“Intervention” is deliberately broader than “technology.” It may include:

- medicines or biological interventions;
- feed or breeding changes;
- materials, equipment and housing designs;
- sensing and environmental-control systems;
- management practices; and
- operational or early-warning systems.

## Current methods

| Method | Use it when | Output |
|---|---|---|
| [Direct functional search](direct-functional-search.md) | The required function is already clear. | Candidates matched directly to technical requirements. |
| [Welfare Technology Scanner](welfare-technology-scanner/README.md) | The mechanism needs deeper decomposition to expose distinct intervention targets. | Ranked candidates tied to explicit causal conditions and evidence. |
| [Jev case-analogy retrieval](jev-case-analogy-retrieval.md) | A curated problem-to-solution library is available and cross-domain analogies may reveal additional leads. | Solutions transferred from similar cases as unverified candidates. |

## How the methods relate

The direct search and Welfare Technology Scanner are alternative depths of
functional search. A condition isolated by the Scanner can become the input to
a direct search.

Jev is complementary. It retrieves leads by problem similarity rather than by
direct functional decomposition. Its candidates must pass the same causal,
functional, evidence, safety and feasibility review as candidates from other
sources.

Candidate sets from several methods could eventually be combined, deduplicated
and reviewed together. That integration is optional and should be evaluated
rather than assumed.

## Shared boundary

Every output is a candidate for investigation, not proof of safety or
effectiveness in the target species or setting. See
[shared foundations](../shared-foundations.md) for evidence, review and
evaluation rules.
