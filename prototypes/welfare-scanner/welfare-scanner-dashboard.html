Welfare problem scanner

A plan for a chain of AI agents. Each agent does one job and hands its result to the next. The goal is to take a farm animal welfare problem and find a technology from another industry that could fix it.

Below, one real problem goes through every step: sore feet in broiler chickens.

The pipeline, one step at a time

This pipeline isn't built yet. The example results come from a scan we ran by hand, so treat them as an illustration of what each agent would hand on.
